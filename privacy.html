Portfolio Privacy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

This public portfolio does not use advertising cookies or sell personal information.

Aggregate visit measurement

When a portfolio page loads, it sends a first-party view event to UGC.community. The event contains the portfolio identifier and a coarse source category such as direct, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Copilot, Gemini, or other. It does not send the referring URL, page content, form content, or a cross-site advertising identifier.

The hosting and analytics services may process ordinary connection information, such as an IP address and user agent, for delivery, security, and operational logs.
